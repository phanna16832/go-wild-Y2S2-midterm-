Meet the team

GO WILD Cambodia started from the passion of two local hikers who never spent a weekend indoors.

Always on the trail, they loved exploring hidden corners of Cambodia. Though they began with separate pages, their shared journey led them to join forces and creating GO WILD Cambodia in December 2024, to inspire others to explore the beauty of places and people, travel responsibly, and reconnect with the nature of their homeland.
